ROLE

Act as a Staff Product Designer (Airbnb + Telegram + Uber + Apple Human Interface + Linear).

PRODUCT

NOW is a real-time social app.

Users open the app.

Press one button.

Meet people nearby within minutes.

The experience should feel cinematic.

Opening the app should create excitement.

Think less “dating app”.

Think “real life starts now.”

DESIGN GOAL

Design language:

* Apple precision
* Telegram speed
* Uber clarity
* Linear polish

CORE PRINCIPLES

1. Every screen has one primary action.
2. Maximum three hierarchy levels.
3. One-thumb usability.
4. 60fps animations.
5. No visual noise.
6. Large touch targets.
7. Reduce cognitive load.
8. The app should encourage opening it multiple times per day.
